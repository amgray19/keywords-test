# Suggested-alternatives audit

757 terms. 73 carry alternatives after this pass, 46 were blanked, 638 had none to begin with.

A term with no alternative still gets flagged in a scan. It just does not put words in the writer's mouth.

## Blanked

### wrong (9)

- **disparate impact** — was `intentional discrimination, equal protection`. Disparate impact is liability WITHOUT intent. Offering 'intentional discrimination' as a substitute inverts the standard.
- **disparate-impact liability** — was `intentional discrimination, equal protection`. Same inversion as 'disparate impact'.
- **gender** — was `sex`. Sex and gender are not synonyms; the substitution is a category error.
- **gender identity** — was `biological sex`. 'Biological sex' is not a rewording of gender identity.
- **historically** — was `traditionally`. Ordinary English word. 'Traditionally' means something else, and the term should not be flagged at all.
- **identity** — was `sex`. 'Identity -> sex' is incoherent outside the specific EO context.
- **institutional** — was `organizational, system-level`. Ordinary English word with a different sense in most contexts.
- **political** — was `government-related, policy-related`. Ordinary English word; 'government-related' is not a synonym.
- **racism** — was `ethnic prejudice, bias based on heritage`. 'Ethnic prejudice' redefines the term rather than rephrasing it.

### erasure (34)

- **allyship** — was `partnership, mutual support`. 'Partnership' drops the meaning entirely.
- **antiracist** — was `fair-minded, equitable approach`. 'Fair-minded' does not carry the claim.
- **biases** — was `preferences, tendencies`. 'Preferences, tendencies' removes the criticism the word carries.
- **biases towards** — was `preferences, inclinations`. Same as 'biases'.
- **discriminated** — was `treated unfairly`. Same as 'discrimination'.
- **discrimination** — was `unfair treatment`. A legal term of art; 'unfair treatment' is not equivalent.
- **discriminatory** — was `unjust, biased`. Same as 'discrimination'.
- **equal opportunity** — was `fair consideration`. 'Fair consideration' is a weaker, different claim.
- **ethnicity** — was `heritage, background`. 'Heritage' is not interchangeable with ethnicity.
- **feminism** — was `women's rights advocacy`. Substituting a description for the name of the movement.
- **fostering inclusivity** — was `building connection`. 'Building connection' means something else.
- **hate speech** — was `offensive language`. 'Offensive language' understates a defined category.
- **health equity** — was `fair access to healthcare`. Defined term in public health; the paraphrase loses it.
- **inequalities** — was `imbalances, gaps`. Same as 'inequality'.
- **inequality** — was `imbalance, gap`. 'Imbalance, gap' softens a measured claim.
- **inequitable** — was `unfair`. Same family.
- **inequities** — was `unfairness, gaps`. Same family.
- **inequity** — was `unfairness, imbalance`. Same family.
- **injustice** — was `unfairness`. 'Unfairness' is weaker and not equivalent.
- **intersectional** — was `overlapping factors`. 'Overlapping factors' drops the analytic meaning.
- **intersectionality** — was `overlapping challenges`. Same as 'intersectional'.
- **marginalize** — was `push aside, overlook`. 'Push aside' is a paraphrase, not a usable replacement.
- **marginalized** — was `overlooked, disregarded`. 'Overlooked' understates it.
- **oppression** — was `control, domination`. 'Control, domination' describes a mechanism, not the concept.
- **oppressive** — was `controlling, harsh`. Same as 'oppression'.
- **race and ethnicity** — was `background and heritage`. Same substitution, compounded.
- **racial** — was `ethnic, heritage-based`. 'Ethnic' is a different category, not a synonym.
- **racial diversity** — was `variety of ethnic backgrounds`. Recasts race as ethnic background.
- **racial identity** — was `ethnic background`. Recasts race as ethnic background.
- **racial inequality** — was `ethnic gaps`. 'Ethnic gaps' drops both the race and the inequality.
- **racial justice** — was `fair treatment`. 'Fair treatment' is not the same claim.
- **racially** — was `ethnically`. Same as 'racial'.
- **sense of belonging** — was `connection, acceptance`. 'Connection' is not the same construct.
- **social justice** — was `fairness in society`. 'Fairness in society' is a paraphrase that loses the term.

### uncited (3)

- **historically received** — was `open to new applicants, competitive on merit`. Same; the replacement is an argument, not a synonym.
- **racial preferences** — was `merit-based selection, individual qualifications`. Drawn from executive-order language; needs the citation to read as reporting rather than advocacy.
- **underrepresented** — was `broad range of applicants, merit-based`. 'Merit-based' as a replacement is a policy position.
